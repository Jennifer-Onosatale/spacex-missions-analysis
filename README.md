# spacex-missions-analysis
SpaceX Missions Data Analysis 2006-2017# SpaceX Missions Analysis 2006-2017

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing 41 SpaceX 
missions from 2006 to 2017 to uncover patterns in launch 
frequency, mission success rates, customer distribution 
and rocket landing technology evolution.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel — Data cleaning and Pivot Table analysis
- SQL — Database querying and validation
- Tableau Public — Interactive dashboard

## Business Questions Answered
1. How has SpaceX launch frequency and mission success 
   rate grown year over year from 2006 to 2017?
2. Which customer country generates the most SpaceX 
   missions?
3. How has rocket landing success rate improved since 
   reusable rockets were introduced?

## Key Findings
- SpaceX grew from 1 launch in 2006 to 10 launches 
  in 2016 — a 900% increase
- Mission success rate improved from 0% in 2006 to 
  90%+ from 2014 onwards
- USA dominates customer base with 56% of all missions
- - Landing success rate reached 100% in 2017 — proving 
  reusable rocket technology mastery

## Recommendations
1. Pursue international customers to reduce 56% US 
   market dependence
2. Use 90%+ success rate to attract new customers and 
   expand into reliability dependent industries
3. Leverage 100% landing success to reduce costs through 
   reusability and offer competitive pricing
